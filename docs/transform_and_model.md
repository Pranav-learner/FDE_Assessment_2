# Step 8G — Transformation & Dimensional Modeling Specification

## 1. Overview & Architecture Flow

The transformation and modeling layer consumes raw ingested Parquet and CSV sources, derives reporting-period boundaries, computes duration metrics, establishes quality flags, and models the data into analytical relational entities.

```text
    raw_trip_data (3,530,109 rows)
          │
          ▼
       transform (period filtering & feature derivation)
          │
          ▼
    reporting_period_trips (3,530,063 rows)
          │
          ├────────────────────────┐
          │                        │
          ▼                        ▼
      fact_trip (3,530,063 rows)  dim_zone (265 rows)
          │
          ▼
     [8H metrics]
```

- **Raw Data (`data/raw/`)**: Read-only, immutable source of truth. Preserved with all original rows and fields.
- **Transformed & Model Data**: Purely derived analytical tables designed for metric calculation.

---

## 2. Reporting Period Definition

- **Target Period**: July 2026 (`2026-07-01 00:00:00 <= pickup < 2026-08-01 00:00:00`).
- **Pickup Timestamp Convention**: A trip belongs to the reporting period based strictly on when it was initiated (`tpep_pickup_datetime`).
- **Dropoff Timestamps Outside July**: Trips initiated on July 31 that finish after midnight on August 1 remain valid July trips. Dropoff timestamp is intentionally **not** filtered.
- **Raw Retention**: The 46 records outside July 2026 (December 2008: 1, June 2026: 7, August 2026: 38) are retained in raw storage and cleanly filtered during transformation.

---

## 3. Operational Feature Derivation

### Trip Duration
- **Formula**: `trip_duration_minutes = (dropoff_datetime - pickup_datetime).total_seconds() / 60.0`
- **Precision**: Fractional minutes preserved as float. Original timestamps are preserved unmodified.

---

## 4. Record-Level Data Quality Flags

Rather than discarding anomalous rows, the pipeline flags records across three dimensions:

| Flag | Logic | July 2026 Valid Count | July 2026 Invalid Count |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `is_valid_duration` | `dropoff_datetime >= pickup_datetime` | 3,530,062 | 1 |
| `is_valid_location` | `PULocationID` & `DOLocationID` in zone reference | 3,530,063 | 0 |
| `is_valid_distance` | `trip_distance >= 0` and non-null | 3,530,063 | 0 |
| `is_valid_trip` | `is_valid_duration & is_valid_location & is_valid_distance` | 3,530,062 | 1 |

### Preservation of Negative Duration Anomaly
The single record with inverted chronology (`pickup = 22:24:27`, `dropoff = 22:24:17`, `duration = -0.1667 mins`) is preserved in the dataset with `is_valid_duration = False`. It is **not** deleted, zeroed out, or artificially repaired.

---

## 5. Dimensional Entities

### Dimension: `dim_zone`
- **Grain**: One row per taxi zone.
- **Rows**: 265 rows.
- **Schema**:
  - `LocationID`: Primary key (Integer, unique, non-null).
  - `Borough`: NYC borough name.
  - `Zone`: TLC zone name.
  - `service_zone`: TLC administrative service zone.

### Fact: `fact_trip`
- **Grain**: One row per completed taxi trip in July 2026.
- **Rows**: 3,530,063 rows.
- **Surrogate Key**: `trip_id` — a deterministic, unique, sequential integer (1 to 3,530,063) generated by the pipeline.
- **Schema**:
  - `trip_id`: Primary surrogate key (Integer).
  - `pickup_datetime`: Trip start timestamp (Timestamp).
  - `dropoff_datetime`: Trip end timestamp (Timestamp).
  - `pickup_location_id`: Foreign key -> `dim_zone.LocationID`.
  - `dropoff_location_id`: Foreign key -> `dim_zone.LocationID`.
  - `trip_distance`: Trip distance in miles (Float).
  - `trip_duration_minutes`: Derived duration in minutes (Float).
  - `is_valid_duration`: Boolean flag.
  - `is_valid_location`: Boolean flag.
  - `is_valid_distance`: Boolean flag.
  - `is_valid_trip`: Composite boolean flag.

---

## 6. Referential Integrity

Both `fact_trip.pickup_location_id` and `fact_trip.dropoff_location_id` map directly to `dim_zone.LocationID`:
- Unmatched pickup references: **0**
- Unmatched dropoff references: **0**

---

## 7. Explicit Exclusions (What is NOT Modeled)

Entities not supported by source facts are intentionally omitted:
- Drivers, vehicles, and medallions
- Passengers and passenger demographics
- Dispatchers and dispatch events
- Traffic, route GPS waypoints, and weather events
- Trip cancellations
