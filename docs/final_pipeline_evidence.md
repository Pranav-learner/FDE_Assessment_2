## A. Business Problem

Taxi operations leadership requires an authoritative, repeatable, and auditable operational intelligence pipeline to evaluate:
1. **Trip Activity**: Overall trip demand and passenger volume patterns across New York City.
2. **Operational Efficiency**: Trip duration and distance benchmarks across pickup locations.
3. **Data Quality & Integrity**: Objective measurement of data quality anomalies (e.g. invalid trip timestamps, corrupt chronologies, or unmapped geographic references) to ensure downstream decisions rely on verified facts.

## E. Business KPIs

The pipeline computes four core operational efficiency KPIs:

1. **Trip Volume**: Total count of completed taxi trips originating within the reporting period.
2. **Average Trip Duration**: Arithmetic mean of duration (in minutes) for trips with valid chronology.
3. **Median Trip Duration**: 50th percentile trip duration (in minutes) for trips with valid chronology.
4. **Average Trip Distance**: Arithmetic mean of distance (in miles) for trips with valid, non-negative distance values.

---

## F. Data Quality KPIs

The pipeline tracks two data governance and quality KPIs:

1. **Invalid Trip Duration Rate**: The percentage of reporting trips exhibiting corrupt chronology ($\text{dropoff\_datetime} < \text{pickup\_datetime}$).
2. **Invalid/Unmatched Location Rate**: The percentage of reporting trips with pickup or dropoff location IDs unmapped to the official TLC Taxi Zone reference.

## I. Unknowns & Unobserved Factors

The pipeline operates strictly on observed telemetry and billing records. The following causal factors are **not** present in the source data:
- **Traffic Congestion Causes**: Specific bottlenecks, accidents, construction, or road closures causing prolonged duration cannot be determined.
- **Weather Conditions**: Precipitation, snow, or extreme temperature impacts are not recorded.
- **Driver & Passenger Identity**: All records are anonymized; driver shift duration, driving experience, and passenger demographics are unobserved.
- **Root Cause of Chronology Anomaly**: The specific meter malfunction or manual timestamp entry error that caused the single negative duration trip is unknown.

---

## J. Core Assumptions

1. **Reporting Partition**: Pickup datetime is the authoritative boundary for allocating trips to reporting periods.
2. **Referential Validity**: A trip location is valid if and only if its integer `LocationID` exists in the official TLC 265-zone lookup table.
3. **Distance Validity**: Distances $\ge 0.0$ miles are structurally valid. A zero-distance trip is not automatically invalid (e.g. meter drop where trip was cancelled prior to travel).
4. **Preservation of Anomalies**: The 1 invalid chronology record is retained in `fact_trip` with `is_valid_duration = False` and `is_valid_trip = False`, rather than silently deleted. This preserves raw operational auditability while safeguarding metrics.

---

## K. Limitations

1. **Single Reporting Period**: This pipeline run reflects July 2026 operations; seasonal trends across full quarters or years require multi-month batch execution.
2. **Referential vs. Physical Truth**: While all location IDs match the lookup table, the data cannot verify whether a driver physically initiated the trip within the recorded polygon.
3. **Extreme Distance/Duration Outliers**: Trips with extreme values (e.g. 100+ miles or several hours) that satisfy positive duration and non-negative distance rules are retained in the reporting dataset.

## L. Reliability Architecture & Evidence

The pipeline implements three core reliability mechanisms:

### 1. Bounded Retry for Transient Faults (Step 8F)
- Transient transport failures (HTTP 500, HTTP 429 rate limit, network timeout) are retried up to 3 attempts using exponential backoff with jitter.
- Proven via simulation: 10/10 test passes without infinite loops.

### 2. Idempotent Reruns (Step 8J)
- Execution is strictly partitioned by the logical run key (`2026-07`).
- Rerunning `python run_pipeline.py --run-date 2026-07-31` rebuilds the dataset atomically via `os.replace`.
- Proven: Re-execution preserves row count (`3,530,063`), unique `trip_id` count (`3,530,063`), and produces identical SHA-256 logical fingerprints (`67fcb576...`).

### 3. Immediate Failure on Critical Validation Breaches (Step 8K)
- Structural and schema failures (e.g. missing `trip_distance`, empty source, or negative distance) are non-retryable.
- The pipeline aborts immediately at the validation gate, logs an `ERROR` entry, suppresses transformation and modeling, and writes zero output files. Existing valid outputs remain 100% untouched.
